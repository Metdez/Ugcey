# Product & operations

Ugecy is an AI-assisted UGC operations platform: it discovers content patterns, turns findings into original creative briefs and scripts, coordinates asset production, and supports reviewable publishing workflows across approved social accounts.

## Core loops

1. Research public, permitted examples and extract explainable content patterns.
2. Produce original hooks, scripts, briefs, and campaign variants for human review.
3. Create or coordinate media through approved tools such as Higgsfield.
4. Queue, approve, publish, and measure campaigns on connected accounts under platform rules.
