# Short-form script structure

## Beat grammar
HOOK (stop the scroll) -> CONTEXT or PROBLEM -> OPEN LOOP -> BODY (demo, story, proof; re-hook every 5 to 8 s) -> PAYOFF (loop closed) -> CTA -> LOOP-BACK (last frame flows into the first).

## Timings
| Length | Hook | Middle | Payoff + CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 s | 0-3 | problem + demo 3-11 | 11-15 |
| 30 s | 0-3 | problem 3-9, demo and proof 9-23 | payoff 23-27, CTA 27-30 |
| 45 s | 0-3 | problem 3-10, demo 10-24 (re-hook near 20), second proof 24-38 | payoff 38-42, CTA 42-45 |
Spoken pace is about 2.5 words per second: 15 s is 35 words, 30 s is 70, 45 s is 105. Over that, cut words, not speed.

## Hook rules (0-3 s)
- One idea; the first meaningful word or visual lands within 0.5 s.
- It must make sense muted: on-screen text carries the idea, and it adds a different angle than the spoken line.
- Test three lenses: Who is this for? What do I get? Why now?
- Variants differ in hook type (curiosity_gap, pov_relatable, negative_warning, result_first, callout_question, contrarian, comparison_us_vs_them, listicle_number), not just wording. Three to five variants, each a different mechanism. Keep the body identical when you want a clean hook A/B test.

## Retention devices
Open loop ("the third one surprised me"), countdown or numbered list, re-hook (a new fact or visual shift), payoff delay, loop ending, captions burned in, pattern interrupt at the midpoint, a visible product in the first 6 seconds on ads.

## On-screen text
Max 7 words per card, high contrast, in the safe zone (not under the bottom 20 percent or the right edge UI). One card per beat. Do not repeat the voiceover word for word.

## Shot list
Per beat: shot type (selfie close-up, over-shoulder, macro product, screen recording, b-roll), action, setting, camera (handheld, tripod), light. Change visuals every 1.5 to 3 seconds. Native look beats polish: real rooms, natural light, slight imperfection.

## CTA
Organic: soft ("save this for later", "send to someone who..."). Paid: explicit but single ("tap to shop"). One CTA only. Sends and saves are stronger distribution signals than likes, so write CTAs that invite them.

## Format picker (by objection)
testimonial (can I trust it) and problem_solution (do I need it) are the most reliable starts. before_after (does it work), tutorial_demo (how does it work), us_vs_them (why this one), pov_skit (is this for me), green_screen_reaction (is the claim real), unboxing (what is it like).

## Script file layout
Header (hook_type, format, length, platform, pattern adapted), beat table with timings, spoken lines, on-screen text, shot list, audio notes, caption with disclosure, risk notes.

## Guardrails
No fabricated first-person experience, no health or income claims without substantiation, no copied wording from sources. Note what must be disclosed (see ftc-disclosure).
