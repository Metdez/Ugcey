# UGC hook and pattern extraction

## Goal
Capture the mechanism that made a post work, as a brand-agnostic template. Wording, characters, music and distinctive bits are never transferred.

## Step 1: is it an outlier?
- Outlier ratio = views / median views of the same account's last 20 posts (exclude the target). 3x is an outlier, 10x is strong. Write the baseline method next to the number.
- Normalise for age: compare views in the first 48 hours, or compare to the baseline at equal age.
- Reach beyond audience = views / followers. Shares per view approximates sends per reach (the strongest distribution signal on Instagram). Saves per view marks utility content.
- One outlier is an anecdote. Several outliers sharing a hook type and format in one niche is a pattern; cluster before concluding.

## Step 2: classify the hook (pick 1 or 2)
curiosity_gap (withheld answer), pattern_interrupt (odd visual or sound in frame 0), negative_warning (stop doing X), contrarian (popular belief is wrong), result_first (outcome then how), callout_question (if you have X), pov_relatable (POV text), storytime (stakes in the first line), listicle_number (3 things), challenge_test (I tried X for 30 days), social_proof_authority (credential or number), comparison_us_vs_them ($5 vs $50), sensory_asmr (macro texture, foley), price_value_shock (price token in 2 s).

## Step 3: dissect the first 3 seconds
Second by second: what is on screen, what is said, what text appears, what sound. Note time to the first meaningful word or visual, and whether it works muted. Name the open loop (the promise held back) in one line, or `none`.

## Step 4: label beats
Roles: hook, context, problem, agitate, open_loop, demo, proof, objection, reveal, payoff, cta, loop. Give start and end seconds and a one-line summary. Count cuts per 10 seconds and mark each re-hook (new fact, visual change, question). Healthy short-form re-hooks every 5 to 8 seconds.

## Step 5: format and production
Format: testimonial, problem_solution, unboxing, before_after, tutorial_demo, pov_skit, street_interview, us_vs_them, green_screen_reaction, day_in_life, founder_story, text_on_screen_ugly_ad. Record talent (on camera, voiceover, faceless, avatar), camera style, caption style and length.

## Step 6: why it works
Each claim names the mechanism (loss aversion, curiosity, identification, proof) and points to evidence: a beat, a timestamp or a metric. Give confidence 0 to 1. If you cannot point to evidence, lower the confidence; do not invent a reason.

## Step 7: transferable template
Write the beat outline with slots, for example: `[0-2s] callout to [audience] with [problem]; [2-8s] show [common wrong fix] failing; [8-20s] demo [product feature] solving it; [20-27s] payoff: [result]; [27-30s] soft CTA`. Then list `do_not_copy` explicitly.

## Quality bar
- Every claim has a source URL and capture date. Metrics you could not verify are `unknown`.
- Note disclosures seen (#ad, paid partnership, AI label), claims made, music rights risk, minors present.
- Never store a transcript verbatim in the card; paraphrase.
