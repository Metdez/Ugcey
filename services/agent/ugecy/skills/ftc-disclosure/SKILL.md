# FTC disclosure and claims

This is operating guidance, not legal advice. When unsure, flag for human review.

## When disclosure is required
Any material connection between the brand and the person speaking: payment, free product, affiliate commission, employment or ownership. If the viewer would be surprised to learn of it, disclose.

## How
- Clear and conspicuous, in the video itself and the caption. Not only in a hashtag pile or after "...more".
- Put `#ad` or "Paid partnership with [brand]" in the first line of the caption, and say or show it in the video (on-screen text in the first seconds for ads).
- Acceptable terms: `#ad`, `Ad:`, "Sponsored", "Paid partnership". Not enough on their own: `#sp`, `#collab`, `#thanks[brand]`, `#affiliate` buried in a list.
- Use the platform's branded-content toggle as well; it does not replace the caption.

## Fake testimonials (16 CFR 465, effective 2024-10-21)
Prohibited: reviews or testimonials by someone who does not exist, or by someone who did not actually use the product, or that misrepresent the experience.
- An AI avatar, synthetic voice or fictional character must not say "I used this for a month" or "it changed my skin". 
- Compliant alternatives: a clearly framed demonstration, an explainer, a skit or POV, a product feature walkthrough, or footage of a real customer with documented consent and real experience.
- Do not generate fake ratings, review counts, "10,000 sold", or fake before/after results.

## Claims
- Objective claims need substantiation on file before the ad runs.
- Health, medical and weight-loss claims (cures, treats, prevents, lose X pounds) are out of scope for this studio. Never approve them. Cosmetic language must stay cosmetic ("feels hydrated", not "heals eczema").
- Earnings or results claims need typical-results context. "Guaranteed" needs terms.
- Comparative claims need a fair basis; do not disparage competitors by name without substantiation.

## AI-generated content
- Disclose realistic AI media using each platform's label (see platform-publishing-checklist). Honest framing in the caption helps ("made with AI").
- Do not use real people's likeness without recorded consent. Avoid minors in promotional content.

## Caption template
`#ad [Brand] sent me this. [One honest line.] [CTA]` for gifted content; `#ad Paid partnership with [Brand]. [Line.]` for paid. For AI-made demos add: `Made with AI.`

## Review checklist
1. Is there a material connection? Disclosed in the first line and in the video?
2. Does anyone claim personal experience who did not have it?
3. Is every factual claim in the allowed list?
4. Is AI media flagged?
